Our story

Franksomi started in 2015 as an electronics repair workshop in Meru. Today we repair phones and laptops, sell accessories, install CCTV and electrical systems, build networks, support institutions and run Landlord WiFi.

We became a registered company: Franksomi Solution Ltd was incorporated on 2 October 2020. We are registered on KRA eTIMS and issue eTIMS invoices.
